Benefits during and after pregnancy in Virginia

Find help with food, rides to medical visits, and questions about your Medicaid coverage.

  • Get help buying food (WIC) Healthy food during pregnancy and for your baby up to age 5.
  • Get a ride to a medical visit Medicaid can give you a ride if you have no other way to get there.
  • What else does my Medicaid cover? Dental care, doulas, breast pumps and more.
  • How long does my coverage last? It depends on your program. See how long yours lasts.
  • How do I report my baby's birth? Add your baby to coverage after birth.
  • Other help for your family Cash help and other programs for families.

Did you know?

Medicaid covers doulas, dental care and breast pumps during pregnancy and after. Many people don't know this.
