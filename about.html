About Steady Start VA

Why we built this

We built Steady Start VA because we saw a need. Many families who qualify for help during pregnancy never get it.

Nationally, only about half (opens in a new tab) of pregnant people who qualify for WIC take part.

Among pregnant people who already have Medicaid and qualify for WIC, fewer than 1 in 5 are enrolled (opens in a new tab).

Many pregnant Medicaid members also don't realize their dental care is covered (opens in a new tab).

The information exists, but it is spread across many agencies and written in hard language. This site brings the most useful parts together in plain words, with a link to the official source on every page.

Who we are

A student-led service project at Virginia Tech, supported by a Ut Prosim Student Impact Grant from VT Engage.

How we keep it accurate

Every answer links to the official source and shows when it was last checked.
