Get help buying food (WIC)

WIC gives you healthy food during pregnancy and for your baby up to age 5. It also offers breastfeeding support.

If you have Medicaid or FAMIS, you already meet WIC's income rule. The WIC office checks the other requirements at your first appointment.

Contact your local health district's WIC office

Find your local WIC office

You can also start online on the Virginia WIC website.

What to say

"I'm pregnant, I have Medicaid, and I'd like to make my first WIC appointment."

How to start

  • Contact your local health district's WIC office, or start online on the Virginia WIC website.
  • Bring your Medicaid card. It counts as proof of income.
  • Ask what else to bring when you make the appointment.

SNAP (food stamps)

SNAP is separate from WIC. It puts money on a card for groceries. You can have both.

Apply for SNAP by phone

Call1-833-522-5582

Or apply online at CommonHelp, or at your local Department of Social Services.
