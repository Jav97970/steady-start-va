Cash help for families

Cash help (TANF)

Monthly cash help for families with children who have low income, to help with basic needs.

How to get it

Apply online at commonhelp.virginia.gov or by phone:

Call to apply for cash help (TANF) 1-855-635-4370

You can also apply at your local Department of Social Services.

What to say

"I'd like to apply for cash help for my family. Can you help me start?"

Good to know

You can apply for SNAP, TANF and health coverage in one application on CommonHelp.

Source: Virginia Department of Social Services, TANF
