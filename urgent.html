Urgent help

Medical emergency

Call911

If something feels wrong with your body during pregnancy or after birth, call 911 or your doctor or midwife right away.

Feeling sad, anxious or overwhelmed

Call or text1-833-852-6262

National Maternal Mental Health Hotline (1-833-TLC-MAMA). Call or text. Free, 24/7, in English and Spanish.

Thinking about hurting yourself, or in crisis

Call or text988

Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text, any time.

Source: HRSA, National Maternal Mental Health Hotline
