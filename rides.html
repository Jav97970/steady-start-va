Get a ride to a medical visit

If you have no other way to get to a covered medical, behavioral health or dental visit, Medicaid can give you a ride.

Call the transportation number on your health plan card

The number is on the back of the card. Call as early as you can.

What to say

"I have Medicaid and I need a ride to a medical visit."

Don't have your card or don't know your plan?

Call the Cardinal Care Managed Care Helpline 1-800-643-2273

If a ride doesn't arrive, get help from Virginia Medicaid transportation services.

Source: Cover Virginia, Pregnancy and Postpartum Coverage
