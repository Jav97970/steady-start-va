Apply for health coverage

Virginia has free health coverage for pregnant people who meet income rules. FAMIS Prenatal Coverage is available regardless of immigration status, and you don't need a Social Security number.

Apply as early in your pregnancy as you can. If you're approved, coverage starts the month you apply.

Apply or ask questions

Call Cover Virginia 1-855-242-8282

Cover Virginia. Choose the Specialized Pregnancy Unit. You can also apply online at CommonHelp.

What to say

"I'm pregnant and I'd like to apply for health coverage."

The income limits are listed on Cover Virginia's pregnancy page.

Good to know

  • A pregnant person counts as two people when Virginia checks income (more with twins). This means a higher income limit.
  • Medicaid may pay medical bills from the months before you applied. Ask about this when you apply. This does not apply to FAMIS Prenatal Coverage.
  • Virginia aims to decide pregnancy applications quickly once it has all your information. If you haven't heard back after a week, call and ask whether anything is missing.

Sources: Cover Virginia, Pregnancy and Postpartum Coverage · Virginia Department of Social Services, Benefits
