How long does my coverage last?

It depends on which program you have.

Not sure which program you have?

Call1-855-242-8282

Cover Virginia. Choose the Specialized Pregnancy Unit.

ProgramCoverage lasts until
Medicaid for Pregnant Women12 months after your pregnancy ends
FAMIS MOMS12 months after your pregnancy ends
FAMIS Prenatal Coverage60 days after your pregnancy ends

Your exact end date is on your letter from Virginia. Call to confirm it.

What to say

"I'm on pregnancy coverage. Can you tell me which program I have and when it ends?"

When this coverage ends

When this coverage ends, Virginia checks whether you qualify for other coverage and sends you a letter. Keep your address up to date so you get it.

Source: Cover Virginia, Pregnancy and Postpartum Coverage
