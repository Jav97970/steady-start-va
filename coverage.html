What else does my Medicaid cover?

Virginia Medicaid and FAMIS cover these at no cost to you:

  • Doctor or midwife visits, before and after the birth
  • Prescriptions
  • Dental care
  • Labor and delivery, including the hospital stay
  • Doula services
  • Breast pumps and breastfeeding support
  • Help for depression or anxiety, and help to quit smoking, alcohol or drugs

Extra benefits from your health plan

Each Medicaid health plan offers extra help for pregnant members. Call the Member Services number on your health plan card and ask what you can get.

Not sure which health plan you have?

Call the Cardinal Care Managed Care Helpline 1-800-643-2273

What to say

"I'm pregnant and I have Medicaid. What extra help can I get from my health plan?"

Doula support

A trained doula can support you during pregnancy, birth and after. Virginia Medicaid members who are pregnant or gave birth in the last 12 months can get community doula services.

How to get it

Ask your doctor, midwife or nurse practitioner to fill out the Medicaid Doula Care Form, then give it to your doula. Get the form: Medicaid Doula Care Form (PDF).

Good to know

You can ask for the form even before you have chosen a doula. It is not a prescription.

Source: Virginia DMAS, Community Doula Program

Last checked: November 2026

Dental care

Medicaid covers dental care during pregnancy and after, such as exams, cleanings, X-rays and fillings.

How to get it

Use your Medicaid ID card at a dentist who accepts Virginia Medicaid. For help finding one, call the Medicaid dental program:

Call the Medicaid dental program 1-888-912-3456

Good to know

Many pregnant members don't know dental care is covered.

Source: Virginia DMAS, Dental

Last checked: November 2026

Rides to appointments

If you have no other way to get to a covered appointment, Medicaid can give you a ride. See how to get a ride.

Had Medicaid before you got pregnant?

Tell Cover Virginia you are pregnant, so you get pregnancy coverage. Call 1-855-242-8282 and choose the Specialized Pregnancy Unit, or report it in your CommonHelp account.

Source: Cover Virginia, Pregnancy and Postpartum Coverage
