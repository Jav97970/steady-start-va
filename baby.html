How do I report my baby's birth?

Report the birth as soon as you can. Your baby needs to be added to coverage. Call Cover Virginia or your local Department of Social Services. You can also ask the hospital or your health plan whether they have reported it.

Report the birth

Call1-855-242-8282

Cover Virginia. Have your baby's name and date of birth ready.

What to say

"I have Virginia Medicaid and I want to report my baby's birth."

"The hospital said it reported the birth. Can you check that my baby was added?"

If you have Medicaid or FAMIS MOMS, your baby is covered until age 1. If you have FAMIS Prenatal Coverage, your baby goes through a quick review, so reporting the birth matters even more.

Keep using your own coverage

Your coverage continues after the birth, including postpartum visits. Check how long it lasts.

Get food help for your baby

WIC covers babies and young children too. See how to get WIC.
